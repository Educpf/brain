

When designing MAS a core challenge is determining how collective decisions can be reached in societies where individual agents are autonomous and self-interested. Computer science handles this using four main frameworks:

- **Game Theory**: Study of strategy among rational agents
- **Social Choice Theory**: How group decisions are made regarding a set of possible outcomes
- **Mechanism Design**: Designing effective protocols/rules to govern multi-agent systems
- **Negotiation**: Finding mutually beneficial deals when agents have conflicting objectives
