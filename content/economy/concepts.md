



# Main Financial Statements

| Document                               | Reason                                    | Explanation                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Profit & Loss (P&L) / Income Statement | Did we make money?                        | Show company's renevue, costs and expenses over a period of time                                                          |
| Balance Sheet                          | What is our financial position rihgt now? | Snapshot of what company owns ( assets ), owes (liabilities) and is worth to owners ( equity )                            |
| Cash Flow Statemnet                    | Where did money actually go?              | Money movements broken into sections: operating(day-to-day), investing(buying/selling assets), financing(loans,dividends) |
| Changes in Equity                      | How did ownership value change?           | How owner equity portion of the balance sheet changed?                                                                    |
