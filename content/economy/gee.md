## Metrics

More basic

### Profitability | Return on sales or production

Net earnings/sales or net income/production (net profit margin)
– EBIT/sales or EBIT/production (operational return on sales)
Return on assets
– ROA = net earnings/total assets
– ROA = EBIT/total assets (operational return on assets)
Return on equity
– ROE = net earnings/equit

### Efficiency

Asset turnover
– Asset turnover = sales or production/total assets
Average inventory period
– [Inventory/cost of goods sold]*365
Average collection period
– [accounts receivables/sales]*365
Average payment period
– [accounts payable/purchases]*365

### Liquidity ( answer to short term )

Current ratio
– Current assets/current liabilities
Quick (acid test) ratio
– [cash+marketable securities+receivables*]/current
liabilities
Cash ratio
– [cash+marketable securities]/current liabilities
– Working capital = current assets - current
liabilities

– Equity/total assets
Debt to equity

– [Equity+long-term debt]/fixed assets
Interest coverage
– EBIT/interest

# SWOT 

Analyze Outside market

- Streghnts, Weakeness, Oportunities, Threats

# PESTE(L)

Analyze Macro environment

- Political & Legal: Government stability, regulations, or market interventions .
- Economic: Income distribution, purchasing power, and macroeconomic growth .
- Social: Demographics, age shifts, and changing consumer behaviors .
- Technological: Innovation velocity and production process updates .
- Environmental: Ecological constraints and resource limitations .
- Legal: changes in regulation and so on

# Porters 5 Forces ( industry atractivness )

Analyze industry 

- Threat of New Entrants: Constrained by entry barriers like economies of scale and capital requirements .
- Threat of Substitutes: Determined by relative price-performance ratios and switching costs .
- Bargaining Power of Suppliers & Buyers: Influenced by volume concentrations and switching vulnerabilities .
- Rivalry among Existing Competitors: Driven by industry concentration and exit barriers .

# Resource View

Internal environment analysis

- Tangible Assets: physical and financial
- Intangible: Non-physical, brand reputation, culture, intellectual property ( hard to duplicate )
- Human Resources: skills, attitude, know-how
- Organization Capabilities: Skills combination turns resources into actual business outcome ( how-to)

# Core Competencies 
Use specific capabilities to create a core competence, defined as:
- It creates clear perceived value for the end customer .
- It is unique and distinct from competitors .
- It is costly or difficult to imitate .

# Marketing 

- **Strategic**: choose the value and plan ( segmentation, target market, value position)
- **Tactical**: provide and communicate after choosing ( price, promotion, distribution etc...)

## Segmentation

Identifying specific groups of customers that share a similar preferences.

**Depth levels**
- **Mass Marketing**: Develop same product for all
- **Segment Marketing**: Creating specific variants for specific segments
- **Niche Marketing**: adapting offers to a narrow specialized group
- **Individual (one-to-one)**: tailor solution to individual. Tech enables mass customization.


**Variables**
- **Geographic**: Regions, climates, preferences
- **Socio-demographic**: Sex, age, income, professional status
- **Psychographic**: Values, lifestyles, activities, opinions etc..
- **Behavioural**: Attitude/knowledge about the product itself ( loyal users, intensive/sporadic etc... )

**Criteria**
- **Measurable**: Traits need to be quantifiable
- **Substancial**: Large and profitable enough
- **Accessible**: Possible to reach 
- **Differentiable**: Traits make them have special interest to pay
- **Actionable**: company has resources to attract them

**Strategies** p vs s
- 1p 1s: ferrari | single segment
- 1p 1+s: cortiça  | product specialization
- 1+p 1s: vista alegre | market specialization
- 1+p 1+p: sonae | selective specialization
- Everything: Pepsico | full market coverage

# Operation

**Management** is using what we have now, **strategy** is defining forward.

## Views of Operation

**Resource View**

Set of real assets found on a balance sheet, broken down into the different types. 
- Sizing: how many resources?
- Timing: when to increase or decrease?
- Type: which ones do we need?
- Location: where do we need them?

**Process View**

How resources perform structured networks 
- Supply: manage them
- Technology: appropriate tools and product design
- Demand: how to match market demand
- Innovation: when to change and so on

**Competencies**

What operation can or cannot do. 
- Cost: total cost of operating
- Time: transofrmation time
- Quality: high-precision product luxuty etc...
- Flexibility: change stuff, inputs, volumes etc...
